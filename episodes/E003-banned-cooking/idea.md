# E003 — idea

**Title:** My Son Banned My Cooking From His Wedding as 'Embarrassing.' Then the Caterer Recognized Me.

Length: 60 min

**Logline:** A 67-year-old widow who ran a family restaurant in Madison, Wisconsin for thirty-four years is told by her son and his rich fiancée that her cooking would be "embarrassing" at their wedding and that she should tell guests she "worked in food service." She learns the star caterer hired for the wedding is her former dishwasher and apprentice, in whose company she is the quiet forty-percent owner. She asks him to say nothing. At the reception her son jokes in his toast that his mother "finally let the professionals cook," and the chef takes the microphone.

- **Villains:** son Gregory Pruitt (39, bank loan officer, ashamed of his childhood in the restaurant) and his fiancée Courtney Ashford (31, event-marketing, status-obsessed). Her father Richard Ashford is NOT a villain: a fair, blunt man who becomes a quiet ally.
- **Humiliation:** text and a lunch conversation banning her cake and food ("embarrassing"); asked to say she "worked in food service"; seated at table fourteen by the kitchen doors; a contract clause that keeps "family" out of the service areas; the toast joke.
- **Event / setting:** engagement lunch → vendor contract → Saturday wedding at a lakeside estate near Madison.
- **Payoff mechanism:** hidden status (silent forty-percent partner in Corso Hospitality, the caterer; the head chef is her apprentice) + a menu built from her own Pruitt's Table recipes. The public reveal is made by the chef, not by her. She does not wreck the wedding; she quietly keeps the forty thousand dollar house gift she planned to give and puts it into a scholarship fund.
- **Allies:** chef Marco Reyes (her ex-dishwasher), his event manager Priya, neighbor Edith, Richard Ashford.
- **Patterns used:** hidden status; wedding / holiday scene; the villain brags and walks into it himself; warm ending (new life: Saturday classes at Corso, the fish fry returns).
- Different from E001 (will/trust, Ohio, Thanksgiving) and E002 (mortgage): different villain mix, different humiliation, mechanism is hidden business ownership and public reveal.
