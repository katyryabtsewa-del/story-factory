## Описание (English)
For six years, Marlene quietly covered most of the payments on her son's house so his wife could call it her dream home. Then, at a party in front of thirty guests, she heard exactly what she was to them. Marlene was a school-district bookkeeper for thirty-four years, and she kept every number in three green ledgers.

What would you have done in her place: forgive and keep paying, or open the books? Tell me in the comments, and where in the world you're listening from.
