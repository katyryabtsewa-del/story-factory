## Описание (английский)
On her seventieth birthday, over prime rib and a candle-lit cake, Peg's daughter slid a glossy nursing home brochure across the table. Page nine was flagged with a sticky note, and the deposit had already been paid. Peg had spent thirty-six years as a 911 dispatcher, the calm voice on the worst night of other people's lives. She did not argue. She asked for the one-week trial stay the brochure offered, and she brought a green notebook.

A quiet, dry-humored story about a woman who writes down the time before she writes down anything else. Would you have forgiven Renata? Tell me in the comments, and tell me where you're listening from tonight.
