# E002 bible — "For Six Years I Secretly Paid My Son's Mortgage"

Setting: Marlow Falls, Ohio (fictional suburb). Present year 2026. Narrator tells it in first person, past tense, warm dry humor.

## Characters (8 named; different initials)
- **Helen Brandt**, 66, narrator. Retired after 29 years as cafeteria manager at Alder Hollow Elementary. Widow. Lives in a rented duplex on Tilden Street, drives a 2009 Buick. Lemon sheet cake is her signature. Reads every invoice and every line of every form (cafeteria manager habit). Keeps a green ledger notebook of every payment.
- **Ray Brandt**, late husband, machinist, died March 2017. Life insurance paid $210,000. Phrase he used: "Paper doesn't forget, Helen."
- **Derek Brandt**, 38, son. Sales rep at Tri-County Flooring Supply, real income $71,000. Soft, avoids conflict, says "Mom, please, not now." Laid off Spring 2020, rehired in 2021 at lower pay. Told Vanessa he does "consulting on the side" for $190,000 a year. Never did.
- **Vanessa Brandt** (née Whitfield), 36, Derek's wife. Runs a home-staging and decor business, "Haven and Hue," with a big social-media following. Image obsessed, smiles while cutting. Calls Helen "Helen" never "Mom". Filed for bankruptcy in 2016 after a failed boutique; she is NOT on the loan or the deed because of that, and believes she is on both (Derek told her "it's all handled"). Told her father (after the 2020 closing) that his 2018 wedding check went into the house; actually it went into Haven and Hue inventory.
- **Piper Brandt**, six on Sep 26, turning seven on Saturday Oct 24 (born 2019), granddaughter. Calls Helen "Nana." Loves lemon cake and counts everything out loud.
- **Gloria Pell**, 67, Helen's best friend of forty years, retired loan officer at Buckeye Valley Credit Union. Blunt, funny, drives a red Subaru.
- **Daniel Okafor**, 54, real-estate attorney on Court Street. Calm, precise, kind. Drafted the 2020 family agreement.
- **Bruce Whitfield**, 70, Vanessa's father, retired car-dealership owner, loves being the benefactor, loud toasts. In 2018 he wrote a $15,000 wedding check; Vanessa later told him it "went into the house" and he loved that story, so he sincerely believes his money is in the house (never saw a closing paper, "wanted to be the man who did it"). (Vanessa's mother is just "Vanessa's mother," Diane, unnamed in text except once as "Missus Whitfield.")

## The money (keep exact)
- House: 418 Larkspur Court, Marlow Falls. Bought November 2020 for $338,000.
- Down payment: $52,000 from Ray's insurance, paid by Helen (November 2020). Loan $286,000, thirty-year, rate three percent.
- Monthly payment: $1,640 (principal, interest, taxes, insurance). Helen pays by automatic transfer from her savings account on the first of every month since December 2020. Seventy-two payments by November 2026 → about $118,000. (Say "seventy-two payments", "more than a hundred and eighteen thousand dollars".)
- Title: Derek Brandt and Helen Brandt as joint tenants with right of survivorship. Loan: Derek and Helen co-borrowers. Vanessa is on neither.
- Current value: $415,000; balance about $248,000; equity about $167,000.
- 2020 Family Agreement (signed by Derek and Helen, drafted by Daniel Okafor, Nov 2020): (1) Helen's $52,000 is repaid first from any sale or refinance; (2) remaining equity split equally; (3) the monthly payments Helen makes are a loan to Derek "to be settled"; (4) after ninety days' written notice Helen may require Derek to refinance in his sole name or sell. Helen insisted on it; Derek signed it laughing ("Mom, it's a house, not a treaty"). Derek also wrote Helen a note on a yellow card: "Mom, thank you. Please don't tell Vee. I'll make it right when I'm back on my feet. Derek."
- At sale (before agent fees and closing costs, which come out of the total first and are shared equally): Helen gets $52,000 + half of the rest ($57,500) = $109,500; Derek gets $57,500. Spoken in ch07 only as "about one hundred ten thousand" and "fifty-seven thousand." Helen forgives everything she has paid and will pay until closing (more than $118,000 at Nov 3; 75 payments by Feb 1) as a gift, in front of Okafor and Derek (Okafor: gift-tax form, she owes nothing), because she will not fight her son over his daughter's childhood. She still takes the money she was owed.
- Refinance alone would cost Derek about $2,200 a month; the bank wants a housing payment under a third of pay (about $1,970 on $71,000), so he cannot.
- Helen's savings: from $210,000 down to about $41,000 (with a little interest) plus pension and Social Security.
- Home-equity line request (the ambush): $95,000 for a pool, a glass-wall kitchen addition and "inventory" for Vanessa's business. Needs Helen's signature as co-owner.

## Timeline
- Nov 2020: closing. Derek laid off in April 2020. Vanessa pregnant earlier (Piper born Oct 2019, so age 1 at closing). Vanessa pressed for a "real house for a real family."
- Saturday September 26, 2026: dinner for the Whitfields at Larkspur Court. Cold open: Vanessa tells Helen in the kitchen she is not family; party is "family only."
- Next week: Helen's Saturday visits with Piper are cancelled; house key returned; Piper phones from Derek's phone.
- Thursday October 8: Helen confronts Derek at Tri-County Flooring; he confesses the lies; asks her to sign the equity line.
- Thursday October 15: ambush at Helen's duplex; Vanessa comes in, drops the act. Helen reads the application: it lists Derek's income as $190,000 and lists Vanessa as co-owner; she learns Vanessa told Bruce the down payment was the Whitfields' wedding gift.
- Friday October 16: Helen visits Daniel Okafor; he serves written notice (Oct 16 → ninety days → January 14); Helen also refuses the equity line in writing. Gloria helps pull the 72-transfer statements.
- Saturday October 24: Piper's seventh birthday party at Larkspur Court. Reckoning.
- Tuesday Nov 3: meeting at Okafor's office; Helen's terms. House listed Nov 16; under contract first week of January; closes third week of February at $418,500. Helen keeps paying to protect her own credit as co-borrower (Okafor says so in ch05 and ch07). Notice deadline Jan 14 = date by which they must have a contract or refinance.
- Epilogue: March, the following spring. Helen buys/rents a small yellow cottage on Linden Lane near Alder Hollow Park; Piper on Saturdays.

## Style notes unique to this story
- Recurring detail: the first-of-the-month "ding" of the autopay notification on Helen's phone. Max 4 uses.
- Lemon sheet cake (Helen brings one to the party; Piper cuts it).
- Do not mention E001 elements (trust, will, Thanksgiving, competency).
- No violence. Vanessa is cruel with words and smiles, not shouting.
