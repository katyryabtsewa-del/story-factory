# E003 — Outline (8 chapters, about 1,150 words each)

1. **Not That Kind of Wedding.** Cold open: the text on Nora's phone while she's rolling kringle dough at midnight in March. The engagement lunch at Verdant: Courtney says "embarrassing," Gregory stays silent, then texts. Courtney: "just say you worked in food service." Nora smooths the tablecloth, says "Of course," and goes home. Promise: "What none of them knew was whose name was on the caterer's paperwork." (Hint at the end but not the full reveal.) Who she is: Pruitt's Table, Sam, Spaight Street. Hook: she hears the caterer's name from Gregory next time.
2. **Sam's Last Good Year.** Edith and lemon bars; history of the restaurant, Gregory in the back booth, "Fish Fry Greg," what she didn't see then. April: the invitation, table fourteen. She'd planned a forty thousand dollar house gift in a card (Sam's life insurance and her own savings). Gregory calls to ask about her health, not about her. Hook: at Sunday dinner he brags the caterer is "Corso Events, booked two years out."
3. **Corso.** The reaction: she says nothing at dinner. Tells how Marco came at nineteen with a stolen-looking duffel bag; teaching him to break down a chicken; the sale of the building; the hundred eighty thousand dollars; forty percent. She drives to Williamson Street, back booth, Marco. Priya brings the Ashford contract: clause nine. Marco furious on her behalf. Hook: Marco says "Chef, say the word and I'll walk out of that contract" and she says the opposite.
4. **Don't Change One Thing.** Nora's decision (a calm one): no sabotage. She asks only that the Ashfords get the best meal of their lives and that nobody tell them anything. Marco: "Then I'm cooking your food." Plan: Marco will serve Nora's menu under Corso names; she'll go as a plain guest, wearing the navy dress. Edith: "You are going to sit at table fourteen and eat your own pot roast?" August tasting: Priya reports Courtney's remarks ("so elevated") about Nora's gravy. Hook: Richard Ashford calls Nora the week before to ask what sort of dress to wear; he's the only one who does.
5. **Table Fourteen.** The wedding day to the toasts: the lawn, the ceremony (Gregory's hands shake), Courtney's friends ask what she does ("food service"), Richard sits by her at cocktails, asks about the pot roast. Dinner: the salad, the short rib arrives; Nora watches Courtney praise it; table fourteen guests rave. Marco looks out of the kitchen door and winks. Hook: the best man finishes and Gregory takes the microphone.
6. **The Professionals.** The toast; the joke; laughter; silence at Nora's table. Marco takes the microphone: his story, dish by dish, the forty percent, "Chef Nora Pruitt." Richard stands. Courtney's face. Nora's measured response: she doesn't speak until asked, then three sentences. Hook: Courtney leaves the head table; Gregory follows to the terrace.
7. **What He Said on the Terrace.** Gregory's confession about the back booth and the smell; Courtney's version ("I didn't want to be the girl whose mother-in-law..."); Nora doesn't forgive on cue. The check in the card she hands them: not the check; a recipe card in her own hand and a note. The dance floor; Richard's mustard joke; the night ends with Priya and Marco and the sour cream raisin pie bites. Hook: Monday morning, Nora signs the papers for the scholarship.
8. **Pruitt's Table Saturdays.** November: apology note and pie from Courtney, Gregory washing dishes, Thanksgiving for fourteen, the fish fry returns, Saturday classes at Corso for people over fifty-five, what she thinks of being ashamed of your mother. Final line and a question to the listener (where are you listening from; would you have said nothing like Nora?).
