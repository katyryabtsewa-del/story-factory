# E008 — Outline (60 min, 8 chapters, 1,100–1,200 words each)

Form D: Wednesday to Friday. Humor, warm. Skeleton: cold open → who she is → three waves → midpoint (the tablet) → quiet plan (overnight) → the Friday door → new life.

1. **Twenty-Two Pounds** — Cold open: Friday, ten forty, Dot on the dock in a fleece, drinking cocoa, watching Craig's silver crossover turn into the drive; one line promise: "By then the house had already been sold, the turkey had already been eaten, and nobody in that car knew either of those things." Back to Wednesday morning: the turkey, the Subaru, who she is, the lake house, Frank, thirty-eight Thanksgivings. Craig's call: "small change of plans." Ends: "You'll love the quiet, Mom."
2. **The Quiet** — Wednesday afternoon and night alone, funny and a little bruised: the empty beds she made, the wood stove, the group-chat photos from Pine Ledge (a heated pool, Dana's smile). Amos waves from his dock. Ends: Thursday morning, a text from Craig: "Happy Thanksgiving! We'll swing by Friday to talk about the house :)"
3. **Dinner for One and a Half** — Thursday: she cooks the whole dinner out of stubbornness, takes a plate to Amos; Amos tells her, slowly, about Frank, the cove and the deed clause. Ends: back at the house she finds Craig's old tablet on the kitchen counter lighting up with a notification: "New booking request: Lakeside Cedar Retreat."
4. **Lakeside Cedar Retreat** — Midpoint: the tablet (listing, her bedroom as "primary suite," Pinecrest Commons email, the group chat). Dot reads everything like a chart. No tears: a nurse's calm. The decision comes by a heron, not a speech. Ends: she dials Nan Ouellette at nine p.m. on Thanksgiving.
5. **Nan Ouellette Answers on the First Ring** — Nan, the kitchen, the deed, the first-refusal clause, the price (six hundred and forty thousand, Dot asks for six hundred and fifty). A phone call to Amos at ten p.m., Amos arrives in earflaps, calls his son-in-law. Humor: everyone in a bathrobe. Ends: midnight, Brooke calls: "Grandma, are you okay? I found the listing."
6. **Brooke at Midnight** — Brooke confesses what she knew and didn't; Dot tells her one honest thing; Brooke says she'll try to slow the car; Dot says do not. Overnight: packing the boots, the canoe, Frank's notebooks, the stove stays? (she keeps the stove sold with house, keeps notebooks, canoe). Ends: five fifty a.m., Nan's headlights on the snow.
7. **The Kitchen Table Closing** — Friday: signing at the table with coffee; the sale; the moving truck of Amos's grandson; the house gets new voices (two small kids and a puppy). Dot leaves a note on the mantel (two sentences). Ends: ten thirty-five, crossover in the driveway.
8. **The Door** — The family arrives with pie and a contractor, finds the kids on the dock; Craig's face; Dana's quiet exit ("Craig, you said she was thrilled."); Tyler quiet; Brooke runs to hug her; Dot says one calm paragraph. Then July: Mill Street, the porch, Craig's bad pie, canoe in the yard, the two grandchildren; what she learned, the question to the listener (would you have sold it?) and where they're listening from.
