## Описание (English)
A retired school bus driver sits in a courtroom while her own son asks a judge to say she can no longer manage her pear orchard. His list is long and sounds reasonable. But Vera Halvorsen has spent thirty-seven years counting every child on her bus, twice, and she has been counting a few other things too.

One Tuesday morning. One hearing. One question from the judge.

Would you have called your son back? Tell us in the comments, and tell us where you're listening from.
