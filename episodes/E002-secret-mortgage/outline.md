# E002 outline (60 min, 8 chapters, 1,100–1,200 words each)

1. **Not Family** — Cold open (150–300 words): in the kitchen at Larkspur Court, during the dinner for the Whitfields, Vanessa tells Helen "You're not family, Helen. Family doesn't carry the deviled eggs," Piper's party will be "family only," promise line (I had paid for the floor she stood on for six years and my name was on the deed). Then step back to the dinner: Vanessa introduced her as "Derek's mother, she helps out sometimes"; Bruce's wine-and-wedding-gift talk; Helen eats in the kitchen. Who Helen is (cafeteria, Ray, duplex, Buick), the ding on the first of the month. END: Derek in the doorway heard it, said nothing, picked up a tray.
2. **The Quiet Arrangement** — 2020 backstory: Derek laid off, Vanessa wants a house, Ray's insurance, Okafor's agreement, the yellow card "Don't tell V," six years of the first-of-month ding, the green ledger. Then the shutting out: next Saturday's visit cancelled, key returned by text, Vanessa's "we're building boundaries" post. END: Piper calls from Derek's phone: "Nana, Mommy says you're not coming to my party. Did you do something bad?"
3. **What I Could Prove** — Helen can't sleep; Gloria's kitchen; Gloria asks what happens if she stops; Helen's ledger; the savings down to $61,000; Helen sees Vanessa's post "we built this home with our own two hands, no handouts"; Helen goes to Tri-County Flooring (Oct 8): Derek confesses he told Vanessa he earns $190,000 consulting, that she believes she's on the deed, he's afraid; "I'll tell her after the party." END: "Mom, one more thing. We need your signature on something. It's just paperwork."
4. **Just Sign Here** — Thursday night at the duplex (Oct 15): Derek with folder, Vanessa waits in the car then comes in. Helen reads every page (cafeteria habit): a $95,000 home-equity line, Derek's income listed as $190,000, Vanessa listed as owner. Helen refuses. Vanessa drops the sweet act: "You were never on the invitation list, Helen. We've tolerated you." Vanessa lets slip that her father believes the down payment was the Whitfields' wedding gift and she will not have the story changed. Midpoint turn: Helen puts the pen down and becomes calm. END: Vanessa's parting line "Don't come Saturday," and Helen thinking about the sentence "paper doesn't forget."
5. **The Quiet Plan** — Friday: Gloria and the 72 statements at the credit union (Gloria calls in a favor); Okafor's office: the 2020 agreement, notice of ninety days, joint-tenancy explained in plain words, why she cannot just stop paying (her credit and Derek's), what she wants (the truth and the house in the right names, not revenge). Letter refusing the equity line. Okafor warns: don't ambush, you can't force the party; Helen: "I'm not going to the party to make a scene. I'm going because I was invited." Piper's handmade invitation arrives in the mail (crayon: "NANA COME. I MADE IT MYSELF."). Lemon cake baking. END: Saturday morning she packs the folder next to the cake.
6. **Seven Candles** — The party at Larkspur Court: bounce house, forty guests, Vanessa blocks the door ("this is private"), Piper runs out and hugs Nana; Bruce's toast: "my wedding gift is in every board of this house"; Helen quietly asks Bruce if he'd like to see the deed. Reads the deed, the agreement, the card, the seventy-two transfers. Vanessa: "That's a lie. Derek?" Derek's silence; then he says "It's true." END: Vanessa's face; Bruce quietly asking his daughter, "Vanessa, did I give you a dime?"
7. **Ninety Days** — Same afternoon and after: Helen cuts the cake with Piper before leaving; Vanessa's meltdown; Bruce's cold verdict; Derek tells all (her 2016 bankruptcy is why she is not on the loan; he protected her). Meeting at Okafor's office: Helen's terms (sale or refinance by January 14; repaid down payment plus half equity; and she forgives the 72 payments as a gift for Piper's sake). Vanessa tries a last insult; Helen answers evenly. Derek cries; Vanessa signs nothing but listens. END: Vanessa at the door: "Why would you forgive it?" Helen: "Because a child needs both of her houses to stay standing."
8. **A Smaller House** — January listing, February sale, Larkspur Court sold for $418,500; Derek and Vanessa in a rented townhouse; Derek pays Helen back a little each month by choice; Vanessa's awkward real apology over lemon cake; Helen's yellow cottage on Linden Lane, Saturdays with Piper, the first of the month no longer dings. Wise short line (no sermon). Final address to listeners: would you have forgiven the payments? Where are you listening from?
