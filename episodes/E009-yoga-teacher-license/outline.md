# E009 — Outline (60 min, 8 chapters, 1,100–1,200 words each)

Form A, humor, Southern. Skeleton: cold open → who she is → three waves → midpoint (the overheard call) → quiet plan → the conference room → new life.

1. **The Waiting Room** — Cold open at Greer's Tire and Brake, Thursday, August: Boyd announces in the plastic chairs, ballgame on the TV, coffee machine, that he is leaving for Skye, his yoga teacher ("I'm not growing here"). Mimi's calm; one-line promise: "By Halloween he would have lost his buyer, his crew and his right to sign a single new contract, and I want to be fair to him: he had it coming for forty-one years." Then who she is: the office, the dolphin on the roof, the box fan, thirty-seven years, Boyd's habit of saying "it's just paperwork." Boyd adds: "Tripp's coming Tuesday to look at the books. Just so you're not blindsided." Ends: back at the office alone she looks at the framed certificate on the wall as if for the first time.
2. **Fine Print** — The 2013 exam story (a testing center in Montgomery, twenty-eight-year-olds, a bag of peanut M&Ms). Tuesday: Tripp takes her office; she is "reassigned" to the supply room; she hands over every book with such helpful honesty that Tripp is startled. Crew reactions; Otis. Humor: the box fan moved to the supply room. Ends: Nettie Rhodes calls the office and, hearing a stranger, says: "Put Mimi on. And tell that boy I'll wait."
3. **Sixty Thousand and the Buick** — Boyd's settlement offer on a restaurant napkin-turned-letter (via his lawyer), health insurance dropped; Corinne Doucet's office above the bakery; Corinne asks "What do you hold, on paper, that can walk out the door with you?" and "Did you sign a non-compete?" (no); Mimi realizes what the certificate means. Skye meets her at the grocery store, offers "space." Ends: Mimi goes home, takes the framed certificate off the office wall in her mind, and decides not to do anything yet: "I decided to be the most helpful woman in Alabama."
4. **Discrepancies** — Friday crew meeting: Boyd hints the books show "discrepancies"; the seventy-one-dollar petty-cash tin; Otis stands up with the receipt (his boots). Boyd laughs it off. Saturday at the office alone, Mimi overhears Boyd's phone call to Tripp: "She's a clerk... she'll sign anything... forty-one years, she's never read the fine print... Tuesday the thirtieth, renewal, she signs, Friday we close." Midpoint decision, calm. Ends: she writes one sentence on a sticky note and puts it in her purse: "Tuesday, nine a.m."
5. **Wanda Pickett Has a Laminated Card** — Monday: Mobile, state office building; Wanda explains the rules in general terms with kindness and a candy dish: resignation in writing, sixty-day grace, no new permits, the November exam, one company at a time. Mimi's quiet plan: finish and protect the fourteen open jobs, tell Otis privately, line up her own paperwork (Corinne drafts her resignation letter; effective at the moment she signs); she will not strand a single customer. Humor: Wanda: "Honey, I have seen men cry at that counter. Never the person who read the manual." Ends: Wanda hands her the form; "Be sure of your date."
6. **Tropical Storm Odalys** — The week before: the storm brushes the coast; pools overflow; customers call Mimi's cell, not the office; she and Otis work two nights; Nettie on her porch in a rain poncho says she'll follow Mimi anywhere; Skye shows at the office in a white linen outfit, bewildered by the phones; Tripp is overwhelmed by calls; Boyd in a golf cart. Mimi is the only one who knows where the valves are. Otis: "Miss Mimi, whatever you're fixing to do, I'm in." Ends: Monday night, Boyd leaves a voicemail: "Tuesday, nine sharp, bring a pen, honey."
7. **Bring a Pen** — Tuesday, nine a.m., the conference room: two gentlemen from Houston, Tripp, Boyd in his best golf shirt, Otis at the door. Boyd slides the renewal form over: "Just sign, Mimi, like always." She says she'll be delighted to, politely, and asks the gentlemen if they'd like to know what she's signing. A calm explanation, no raised voice; Tripp turns pale; the Houston men ask who the qualifier is; Boyd says "technicality"; she signs a different paper: the resignation, effective at nine-oh-four. Boyd: "You're doing this to hurt me." Mimi: "No, Boyd. I'm leaving you exactly what you always said I was: a clerk. Clerks can't pull permits." Houston leaves. Otis hands Mimi the cardboard box (mug, the box fan). Ends: in the parking lot Boyd says "Fine, I'll take the test," and Mimi says: "It's November eighteenth. Bring a pen."
8. **Gaudet Blue Water** — What happened next, on ordinary mornings: Wanda's kind "no" to Boyd; the deal officially dead; he fails the exam by four points; crews leave; Skye in January; his customer list sold for three hundred and ten thousand; the house stays hers by plain arithmetic. Boyd on her porch in January asks her to requalify for forty percent; she declines once, kindly. The new company: Nettie's pool first, the pelican on the truck, Otis as partner, one hundred and four customers by April, the framed certificate on the porch wall. Last lines + question to the listener (would you have signed, or told him?) and where they are listening from.
