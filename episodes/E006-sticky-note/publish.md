## Описание (English)
A gray January morning, a notary's conference room, a stack of papers an inch and a half thick, and a pen already in her hand. Josephine Ledbetter, seventy-one, has been a seamstress for forty-one years and has never seen this office before. Then her fingers find a yellow sticky note under the clipboard, written by someone who is risking a job to tell her one thing.

Some people will tell you that you forget things now. Some will pat your hand while they say it. A woman who has spent a lifetime noticing the stitch nobody else sees knows exactly what to do with a pen she has been handed.

Have you ever been handed a pen when what you needed was time? Tell us in the comments, and tell us where you're listening from.
