## Профиль
- **Название канала:** Stories From Grandma's Kitchen
- **Handle:** @StoriesFromGrandmasKitchen
- **Фото профиля:** `avatar_A.jpg` (запасной — `avatar_B.jpg`)

```
Welcome to Stories From Grandma's Kitchen.

Pull up a chair, pour yourself a cup of coffee, and listen. Every story here is told the way a grandmother tells it: slowly, warmly, and with a few secrets saved for the end.

These are fictional family stories about betrayal, quiet strength, and the sweet taste of justice served at the right time. Each one is a long, relaxing listen, perfect for your morning coffee, a drive, a quiet evening, or falling asleep.

New story every week. Subscribe and turn on the bell so you never miss one.

All stories on this channel are works of fiction created for entertainment. Names, characters and events are imaginary, and any resemblance to real people is coincidental. Narration uses a synthetic (AI-generated) voice, and cover images are AI-generated.
```

## Настройки
- Страна: United States · Язык канала: English
- «Для детей»: **нет**
- Ссылки: можно добавить почту в «Контактные данные»
- Рекомендуемое видео для не подписчиков: первый загруженный эпизод (E001)
- Разделы на главной: «Видео» → «Плейлисты». Плейлист 1: **Full Stories (1 hour+)**, плейлист 2 (позже): **Stories for Sleep**

## Обязательно при каждой загрузке
Отметка «Altered or synthetic content» = **Yes**, «Not made for kids», дисклеймер вымысла в описании (уже в `publish.md`).
