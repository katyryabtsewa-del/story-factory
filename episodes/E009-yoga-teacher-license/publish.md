## Описание (английский)
On a Thursday morning in August, in a plastic chair at a tire shop, with a ballgame on the television and a paper cup of free coffee in her hand, Mimi's husband told her he was leaving. She did not scream. She had thirty-seven years of receipts, a very polite voice, and one framed certificate on the wall of the back office that nobody had ever bothered to read.

A dry, funny Gulf Coast story told the way Mimi would tell it on her back porch. Would you have signed, or told him a week early? Tell me in the comments, and tell me where you're listening from and what the weather is like there.
