# E001 — idea

**Title:** My Children Fought Over My Will While I Was Still Alive. So I Invited Them to Hear It Early.

**Logline:** At her husband's one-year memorial lunch, a 69-year-old widow overhears her children dividing her house on a spreadsheet; when they try to have her declared incompetent, she invites them to Thanksgiving to hear her will read early, and one sentence written by her lawyer turns her greediest son's inheritance over to his own daughter.

- **Villains:** eldest son Kevin and his wife Brittany (schemers); middle daughter Rachel (goes along, partly redeemed).
- **Humiliation:** her children divide her belongings while she is in the next room; her daughter-in-law tells her doctor she "leaves the stove on" (she ran a diner kitchen for 31 years).
- **Event / setting:** memorial lunch → Thanksgiving dinner in her own kitchen.
- **Payoff mechanism:** amended living trust with a protective clause — anyone who tries to have her declared incompetent or take her property while she is alive is treated as having died before her; Kevin's share passes to his daughter Olivia. Plus a letter from her late husband read aloud.
- **Allies:** granddaughter Olivia (16), youngest son Tommy, family lawyer Harold Greene.
- **Patterns used (research 2026-11-07):** inheritance + "the lawyer read one sentence"; grandchild ally; holiday reckoning.
