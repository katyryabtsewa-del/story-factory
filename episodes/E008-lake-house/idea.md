# E008 — idea

**Title:** They Left Grandma Alone at the Lake House for Thanksgiving. By Friday Morning, I'd Sold It.

Length: 60 min

**Logline:** A 68-year-old retired school nurse is told by her grown son and his teenage kids that she can "just stay behind" at her own Maine lake house over Thanksgiving while they go to a fancier rental. Over three days (Wednesday to Friday) she quietly, with dry humor, discovers what they planned for the house and sells it before they come back.

**Variety plan (idea 10):** form D (three days), tone with humor, warm ending, villain = one son + teenage grandchildren (not a daughter-in-law), Maine in late November.
