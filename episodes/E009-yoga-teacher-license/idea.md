# E009 — idea

**Title:** My Husband Left Me at Sixty-Five for His Yoga Teacher. He Forgot Whose Name Was on the License.

Length: 60 min

**Logline:** Marilyn "Mimi" Lanier, 65, has spent forty years as the office manager (and, since fifty-two, the state-licensed qualifying contractor) of her husband Boyd's Gulf Coast pool-and-patio company in Alabama. Boyd tells her he's leaving for his yoga teacher in a tire-shop waiting room while a ball game plays on the TV. She does not scream. With dry Southern humor she takes him at his word: she'll gladly step aside, exactly as the paperwork allows.

**Variety plan (idea 11, rewritten per variety.md п.7):** form A, but the villain is a husband (not "the family"); heroine from the Deep South, sharp-tongued and funny (not shy); tone with humor. Because E008 already used "the house/property was already hers," the mechanism is NOT hidden ownership: the business is openly his (his name on the trucks), and what she holds is her *qualifier credential* + forty years of customers' and suppliers' trust + her own personal accounts. Nobody reads a document aloud at a party; no lawyer reveal at the end — the turn comes at a state licensing office counter and on customers' porches.

**Villain:** Boyd Lanier, 68 — charming, vain, sure the "little woman in the back office" is furniture. Plus a smooth young business-partner-in-waiting, not a cartoon: the yoga teacher is shallow but not evil.

**Mechanism:** the company's contractor license has to be held by a named qualifying person who passed the exam — Boyd never passed it; Mimi did, quietly, at 52, to save the company after an inspection scare. When she resigns as qualifier, in writing, on the day the divorce papers arrive, the company cannot legally pull permits or sign new jobs. Meanwhile her long-time customers, crews and suppliers (who always called *her* when something went wrong) have choices.

**Ending:** payback with grace — Boyd begs (not for love, for the license); she declines; she opens a small one-woman outfit with the crew chief who always liked her; new life, a warm last scene. Not "revenge party": consequences land through ordinary Tuesday mornings.
